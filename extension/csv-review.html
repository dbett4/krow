Types, required values and keys

Supported types: string, integer, decimal, boolean, date and timestamp. Unknown types stay incomplete. Required means a value cannot be empty. Key columns are compared as exact text; “001” and “1” remain distinct. Select at most eight keys.

Reporting context · Separate from arithmetic

Without context, totals have no verified period or units. Check exact row labels against your source owner's policy; Wingman never infers currency or converts amounts.

Source-owner policy · Not accounting approval

Empty unit column or unknown basis stays incomplete. Basis is recorded, not validated. No fiscal-calendar, FX, account-mapping, sign-convention or accounting-correctness claim.

Private review journal · Optional

Your operator enabled local storage. Saving retains scope labels, finding locations/hashes and explicit reviewer reasons—not raw CSV. Accepted exceptions remain findings; they never approve accounting or authorize a Workiva write.

Keep workspaces, file copies and periods separate

Labels are your declarations, not authenticated file IDs. Load is historical; run a fresh check before confirming a decision. Clear inputs does not delete the journal.
