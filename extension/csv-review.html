Wingman / Pre-import review

Check a CSV before it reaches Wdata.

Find row-level problems. Keep the evidence for your review.

Private local screen · No Workiva connection

Your inputs go to this VPS process only when you run a check. They are not saved. A local pass does not verify the native schema, import, period, units or accounting.

1. Supply your CSV

Up to 256 KiB, 1,000 data records and 64 columns. Blank records are checked, not dropped.

2. Declare the expected schema

Use the exact column order and types from your approved source. Wingman does not infer them from the CSV.

Types, required values and keys

Supported types: string, integer, decimal, boolean, date and timestamp. Unknown types stay incomplete. Required means a value cannot be empty. Key columns are compared as exact text; “001” and “1” remain distinct. Select at most eight keys.

3. Review the evidence

Start with a check.

Declare your schema, then run the screen. You’ll see affected records, exact numeric totals and checks that remain unknown.

No data is uploaded to Workiva. Nothing is fixed automatically.

The JSON packet includes column names, findings and numeric totals, but not raw CSV rows. Treat it as sensitive. Download is explicit; the packet is not approval.
